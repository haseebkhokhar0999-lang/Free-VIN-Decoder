What a VIN Decode Shows

Decoding a 17-character VIN returns factory-built details such as make, model, model year, trim, body class, engine, fuel type, transmission, drive type, assembly country, and manufacturer.

Factory specs only — accident history, salvage records, odometer readings, liens, and ownership records are not part of a VIN decode. Vehicles built before 1981 or outside the US market may not appear in NHTSA records.

Where to Find Your VIN

  • Dashboard: Lower-left corner on the driver’s side, visible through the windshield.
  • Door jamb: Sticker inside the driver-side door frame near the latch.
  • Documents: Listed on your vehicle registration card, title, and auto insurance policy.
